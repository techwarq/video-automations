How did an AI video startup reach a $200 million annual revenue run rate so insanely fast?

Meet Higgsfield AI.

Instead of making users learn complicated video software, Higgsfield made cinematic AI video creation ridiculously simple.

Creators could generate shots, camera movements, and entire videos in minutes.

And they targeted a huge market: creators, marketers, brands, and agencies that constantly need new content.

That created a powerful growth loop — more content, more sharing, more users.

By January 2026, Higgsfield said it had 15 million users and was generating 4.5 million videos every day.

And that's how a video AI startup went from zero… to a $200M annual run rate.
